Audio Fingerprint Sync · v0.1

Sync anything to audio.

An open file format that pairs audio fingerprints with time cues. Any device with a microphone can know where in this audio it is, in real time, without a network.

Use your own files

Drop in any media file with a matching SRT to test AFS on your own content. If you don't have an AFS file, one will be generated in your browser.

Audio decoding happens in your browser. Files never leave your device. Generated AFS files can be downloaded once ready.

Pick a demo to begin.

One video, two SRTs — one correct, one shifted +2 s. Toggle AFS correction and watch the off subtitles snap into place.

Original clip and an edited version with three short cuts, playing side by side. Same SRT for both. Only the AFS-corrected track stays in sync past the first cut.
03 Cannons
Tchaikovsky's 1812 finale with screen flashes and phone vibration on every cannon hit. Use mic mode to fire haptics from a phone listening to a laptop speaker.
